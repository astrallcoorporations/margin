Privacy

Margin is a study app for a small group of students. Vex is its AI helper. This page says what they do with your information.

Signing in

You sign in with Google. Vex receives your name and email address, and checks that the account is a school (ked.edu.in) account or one of a short list of allowed addresses. Nothing else about your Google account is read at sign-in.

Google Classroom and Drive (only if you connect them)

If you press Connect Classroom, Vex gets read-only access to your courses, assignments, announcements, class materials, and the Google Drive files your teachers attached to them. It uses this to show what is due and to answer your questions about it. It cannot hand anything in, change, or delete anything. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the saved access.

What is stored

A sign-in cookie in your browser, the Google access Vex needs to keep reading your Classroom, and how many daily credits you have used. Your study progress in Margin stays on your own device.

AI processing

When you ask Vex something, your message (and any post or photo you choose to send with it) is sent to Groq, an AI service, to produce the answer. Do not send anything you would not want a service like that to see.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone else. One student cannot see another student's Classroom data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the support email shown on Google's consent screen for this app.
